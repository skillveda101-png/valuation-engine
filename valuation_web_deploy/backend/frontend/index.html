How rates are used

  • Market weight % — share of market rate in FMV (e.g. 70)
  • Govt weight % — share of government rate in FMV (e.g. 30)
  • Distress % — Forced / distress value = FMV × this % (e.g. 80)
  • Belt 2 market factor 66% and Belt 3 market factor 50% are applied inside the calculation engine when belting is used.
